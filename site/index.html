      Method and caveats
      • Trailing PE = price ÷ sum of the last four reported quarterly EPS, using only quarters reported on or before that date (point-in-time).
      • Forward PE = price ÷ next-12-months EPS, time-weighted across fiscal quarters. Today: consensus. Historical dates: actuals reported since (hindsight) plus consensus for any unreported quarters — providers do not supply historical consensus snapshots. Hover a cell for the estimate share.
      • 52-week high/low use intraday extremes over the trailing 365 days, split-adjusted, extended live if today breaks through.
      • Reference dates use the close on or before the date.
      • ETFs: look-through earnings of the top holdings (units held per ETF share fixed at the latest holdings snapshot), scaled for weight coverage. Equivalent to a harmonic-mean PE; loss-makers count. Accuracy falls for dates far back as holdings change.
      • EPS basis: Alpha Vantage reported EPS (Street/adjusted basis) where available; FMP GAAP diluted EPS as fallback — flagged on the row if bases are mixed.
      • Negative or zero EPS shows as n/m.
